# Threshwork — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Content operations director

- As a content operations director, I want every queue scored against weightings we agreed in advance, so that the automation order is defensible to the COO rather than determined by whichever vendor demoed most recently.
- As a content operations director, I want realised savings reported per queue against the business case that funded it, so that I can defend or kill the programme at the next budget cycle with evidence rather than narrative.
- As a content operations director, I want to model what a forty percent volume increase does to each queue's capacity before it arrives, so that I am planning against anticipated demand rather than last quarter's actuals.
- As a content operations director, I want traffic pulled back to human review automatically when post-deployment quality falls below the queue's floor, so that a bad automation decision costs me a week rather than a quarter.

### Queue manager and workforce planner

- As a queue manager, I want to set confidence thresholds per queue by impact tier, so that a low-confidence hate speech call always reaches a human while a low-confidence garment-colour tag does not.
- As a queue manager, I want items approaching a statutory removal clock or a client SLA to surface ahead of everything else, so that a breach is something we chose rather than something we discovered.
- As a queue manager, I want to redeploy agents from automated queues onto the hard residual tail, so that throughput gains are not paid for with quality on the difficult cases.

### Review agent and language QA reviewer

- As a review agent, I want the machine's proposed decision presented with its confidence and the evidence behind it, so that I am adjudicating a proposal rather than starting the item from zero.
- As a language QA reviewer, I want to flag a class of item the classifier is systematically getting wrong, so that the calibration set is corrected once instead of the same error repeating across ten thousand items.
- As a review agent, I want my exposure to graphic material tracked against the policy cap, so that a volume target can never quietly override the wellbeing policy that was written to protect me.

### Vendor and sourcing manager

- As a sourcing manager, I want to run two vendors in shadow on the same live queue against our own labelled sample, so that selection rests on our data rather than on the vendor's benchmark.
- As a sourcing manager, I want per-queue cost per decision that includes classifier spend and the residual human handling it leaves behind, so that the cheaper vendor is cheaper on a total basis rather than on a rate card.
- As a sourcing manager, I want to cut traffic away from a vendor whose accuracy has drifted below the contracted floor and fail over to the tested alternative within the same shift, so that a contractual remedy is not the only thing standing between drift and published harm.

### Policy and regulatory affairs lead

- As a policy lead, I want to approve or refuse automation for a queue with the reason recorded, so that an unstable policy definition cannot be automated by operational enthusiasm.
- As a policy lead, I want a period record per queue showing what was decided by machine, what a human reviewed, what was appealed and overturned, and where content was sent for processing, so that a regulator or an enterprise client receives an answer rather than an investigation.
- As a policy lead, I want content under a client's territorial restriction to be structurally incapable of routing to a vendor outside that territory, so that procurement convenience cannot create a data transfer incident.
